What this check covers

Ratios show two decimals; pass/fail uses full precision. Large text is at least 24px regular or 18.67px bold. The 3:1 graphics/UI check applies to meaningful components against adjacent colors. Also review focus states and meaning conveyed by color. Read the text contrast guidance and non-text contrast guidance.
